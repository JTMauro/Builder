Setup

Wear your usual glasses if you have them. Cover one eye to test separately. Use the arrow keys (or buttons) so you can answer from a distance; Space = "can't tell".

Result

Arm's-length test (near point and age)

Age-related loss of near focus (presbyopia) shows up as a near point that moves away. Do this with one eye covered and no reading glasses. Complete the calibration card above first.

Hold the screen at arm's length so the small text below is sharp. Bring it slowly closer until it first turns blurry. Stop and measure from your eye to the screen with a ruler or tape.
